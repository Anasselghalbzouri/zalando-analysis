# M03 — Campaign (sale event) engine & countdowns

**Status:** Analysed in Phase 02 (2026-11-04). **Scope:** read-only, on the user's logged-in Brave tab. No clicks on "Informez moi", cart or newsletter.
**Labels:** CONFIRMED = directly observed · HYPOTHESIS = plausible, needs verification · UNKNOWN = not observable or not investigated · OUT OF SCOPE = excluded by authorization.
**Paths:** `a11y/…` = `evidence/a11y/…`. Evidence IDs are in [`evidence/INDEX.md`](../../evidence/INDEX.md). Business rules (BR-xx) are in [business-rules.md](../requirements/business-rules.md).

## 1. Module purpose
- **CONFIRMED:** The platform sells through time-limited, named sales called **campaigns** (`/campaigns/{id}`). Each campaign groups a product assortment, shows a maximum-discount headline and an end countdown, and is the parent of every product URL (`/campaigns/{id}/articles/{sku}`).
- **HYPOTHESIS:** The campaign is the unit that controls when a product can be bought, and at what price (flash-sale model).

## 3. Entry points and navigation
- **CONFIRMED:**
  - Campaign tiles on `/event` and on every event landing `/event/{id}` (see M05). The tile heading link goes to `/campaigns/{id}`.
  - Product links in rails ("Recommandé pour vous", "Populaire dans votre région") go straight to `/campaigns/{id}/articles/{sku}`, skipping the campaign page.
  - Search results and brand pages link products the same way.
  - The campaign page breadcrumb is `Accueil (/event) › <campaign name>`.
- **CONFIRMED:** Upcoming campaigns ("Prochainement") have **no link and no ID** in the accessibility tree. The heading is plain text, and only an "Informez moi" button is offered.
- **Evidence:** `a11y/home_scrolled.yaml`, `a11y/page_habitat.yaml` ("Prochainement" list), `a11y/campaign_desktop.yaml` lines 22–30.

## 5. User interactions
- **CONFIRMED:** Click a tile → campaign page. Click a product → PDP within that campaign.
- **CONFIRMED (UI only):** "Informez moi" on an upcoming tile. **Not clicked**: it would subscribe the account, which is a state change → OUT OF SCOPE until authorised (see M04).
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether a tile's image is also clickable (only the heading link appears in the tree).

## 6. Inputs and outputs
- **Inputs (CONFIRMED):** campaign ID from the URL. No other user input on the campaign tile.
- **Outputs (CONFIRMED):** campaign name, image, a countdown to end or start, max-discount percentage or "Special", and the scoped product list.
- **ID format (CONFIRMED pattern, meaning HYPOTHESIS):** every live campaign ID observed is `ZZO` + 4 alphanumerics (e.g. `ZZO4RN5`, `ZZO4P2W`). A special ID `top-picks` is used for the recommendation listing (`/campaigns/top-picks/articles`). The IDs look sequential (HYPOTHESIS).

## 7. Business rules
| ID | Rule | Class | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-03-01 | Every product is addressed inside a campaign: `/campaigns/{campaignId}/articles/{sku}` | CONFIRMED | all product links in every capture |
| BR-03-02 | A live campaign shows an end countdown, "Se termine dans" | CONFIRMED | 126/126 tiles in `a11y/home_scrolled.yaml` |
| BR-03-03 | The countdown shows days + hours + minutes when ≥ 1 day remains, and hours + minutes + seconds when < 1 day remains | CONFIRMED (format) | e.g. "2 jours 5 heures 50 minutes" vs "5 heures 50 minutes 45 secondes" |
| BR-03-04 | The headline is "Économisez jusqu'à* N%", where * = "Par rapport au prix de vente conseillé" (vs RRP) | CONFIRMED | footer footnote in `a11y/page_habitat.yaml` |
| BR-03-05 | Some campaigns show "Special" instead of a percentage. These are price-point or selection campaigns, e.g. "Looks enfant à moins de 40 €", "Mode premium pour enfant à moins de 60 €", "BOSS Kidswear", "Diadora" | CONFIRMED (label) / HYPOTHESIS (rule: used when no single max-discount figure applies) | 5 of 126 on `/event` |
| BR-03-06 | Observed max-discount range is 50–85%, with 75% the most common (57 of 126 on `/event` at ~17:09 UTC) | CONFIRMED (snapshot) | `a11y/home_scrolled.yaml` |
| BR-03-07 | End times cluster: all 126 countdowns in one capture fall into **two offsets mod 24 h** (≈ +5 h 50 and ≈ +23 h 49 at 17:09 UTC), plus whole days | CONFIRMED (clustering) | `a11y/home_scrolled.yaml` (33+39+33+1+1+2 vs 6+5+6) |
| BR-03-08 | Campaigns end at a small number of fixed daily times. Based on the analysis machine clock (17:09 UTC), the two end times are ≈ 23:00 UTC (00:00 Paris) and ≈ 17:00 UTC (18:00 Paris). Arithmetic: 17:09 + 5:50 = 22:59, and 17:09 + 23:49 = 16:58 the next day | HYPOTHESIS (clock arithmetic; the analysis clock matches REA's own capture timestamps, e.g. `2026-11-04T17:11:04Z` in `ev_bda5136d…`) | as above |
| BR-03-09 | A campaign moves from "Prochainement" (upcoming) to live at a scheduled time. The five sales listed as "Démarre dans 1 heure 21 minutes" in Phase 01 were all live in Phase 02, each with ≈ 2 j 23 h left | CONFIRMED (matched **by name only**, because upcoming tiles carry no ID) | Phase 01 `a11y/page_sports.yaml` lines 286–310 → `page_habitat` (Berlinger Haus `ZZO4R0X`), `page_enfant_scrolled` (Kappa + Hummel + Reima `ZZO4QNQ`), `page_homme_scrolled` (Hummel `ZZO4NTL`, Vivobarefoot `ZZO4P57`, CAMICISSIMA + OLYMP + Strellson `ZZO4SCD`) |
| BR-03-10 | Those newly started campaigns run ≈ 3 days, starting and ending at the same daily slot. The help page says 3–5 days on average | CONFIRMED (help text: 3–5 days) / HYPOTHESIS (same start/end slot) | BR-03-09 + countdowns |
| BR-03-11 | Upcoming campaigns can't be browsed before they start: there is no link and no ID in the tile | CONFIRMED (UI) / HYPOTHESIS (rule is enforced server-side) | `a11y/page_habitat.yaml` "Prochainement" |
| BR-03-12 | Close to the start, the upcoming tile shows a relative countdown ("Démarre dans …", seen at 1 h 21 before start). Further out, it shows an absolute day and time ("Démarre le jeu. 5 novembre 06:00", seen at ≈ 13–14 h before start). The switch is somewhere between 1.4 h and 13 h, or the rule is "same day vs a later day" | CONFIRMED (both formats seen) / UNKNOWN (threshold) | Phase 01 sports vs Phase 02 habitat |
| BR-03-13 | One campaign can appear on several event landings at the same time (68 of 123 vertical-listed campaigns appear on 2–4 landings). ## 8. State transitions
```
[Scheduled / Upcoming] --start time reached--> [Live] --end time reached--> [Ended]
   tile in "Prochainement"                tile in grids, countdown        (not observed)
   no link, "Informez moi"                "Se termine dans"
```
- **Upcoming → Live:** CONFIRMED by name (BR-03-09).
- **Live → Ended:** UNKNOWN. No ended campaign was observed. What an ended campaign URL or PDP returns (404, redirect, "vente terminée" page) is not known. I didn't guess old campaign IDs because that would go beyond linked pages.
- **Draft / unpublished:** UNKNOWN (back-office only).

## 9. Dependencies on other modules
- **M05 Event landings:** list and group the campaigns. A campaign can belong to many events (BR-03-13).
- **M04 Upcoming & notify:** shows campaigns in the Scheduled state.
- **M06 Listing:** renders the campaign's assortment with filters.
- **M07 PDP:** shows the campaign countdown again and holds the purchase entry point.
- **M12 Pricing:** the headline % is relative to RRP.
- **M13 Scarcity:** "Dernières heures" may come from the campaign end time (HYPOTHESIS).
- **M10 Recommendations, M08 Search, M09 Brand pages:** all produce campaign-scoped product links.

## 10. Observable validation and error states
- **UNKNOWN:** ended or invalid campaign ID handling, and the empty-campaign state. Neither was observed.
- **CONFIRMED:** no error states appeared in any capture.

## 11. Responsive behaviour
- **UNKNOWN.** Only a 1280×720 desktop viewport was rendered. A 390 px viewport in connect mode did not reflow, and launch mode failed to load (Phase 01, platform-overview §3).

## 12. Unknowns and assumptions
- **LIKELY (BR-03-17, HYPOTHESIS) — timezone of "06:00":** probably rendered in the browser's timezone (UTC under REA), which would be 07:00 Paris. Original note: scenario captures emulate UTC. A passive inspect (`ev_bda5136d…`) also showed "06:00", but it read a page that had been loaded under that scenario. So whether this is 06:00 UTC or 06:00 Paris time is still open. Verify in a fresh tab loaded with no REA scenario.
- **UNKNOWN:** the actual start and end timestamps. The DOM has `<time datetime=…>` elements (attribute present in the passive inspect `ev_bda5136d…`), but REA records only attribute *names*, not values. Reading the values needs a capture that exposes attribute values: a DOM capture with values, or DevTools by the user.
- **UNKNOWN:**
  - how the headline percentage is computed (max of SKUs? curated?)
  - stock allocation per campaign
  - whether campaigns can be extended
  - campaign end behaviour
- **UNKNOWN:** why the campaign page shows no countdown when the PDP does. It may be outside the captured viewport, or there may be a sticky element the tree didn't expose. Check with a screenshot of `/campaigns/{id}`.
- **ASSUMPTION, not used as a rule:** a back-office creates campaigns. This is logged as HYPOTHESIS only.
