## 7. Business rules
- **BR-13-01 CONFIRMED:** low stock is shown per size, and N changes over time (3 → 1).
- **BR-13-02 CONFIRMED (upgraded from HYPOTHESIS):** cart items are *reserved* stock.
  - The empty cart says "Vous n’avez aucun article réservé dans votre panier."
  - The cart groups items under "Réservé".
  - The remove dialog says "Si vous retirez cet article, il sera disponible pour que d’autres acheteurs puissent le réserver."
  - HYPOTHESIS: the "Réservé" tile state means all remaining units are reserved by others.
- **BR-13-03 HYPOTHESIS:** "Dernières heures" means the campaign ends within some threshold.
- **BR-13-04 CONFIRMED (help page):** quantities are limited, and ended or sold-out items aren't reordered ("nous ne pouvons les recommander").

## 8. State transitions
CONFIRMED: Available → Reserved (20 min, in a member's cart) → Available again (removed, or HYPOTHESIS: expired) or sold through checkout (OUT OF SCOPE).
