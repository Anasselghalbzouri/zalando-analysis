## 1. Module purpose
CONFIRMED: The member area for orders, returns/withdrawal, personal data, addresses, newsletter settings, saved size filters, and the link to Zalando Plus rewards.

## 2. User roles and actors
- **Member:** CONFIRMED.
- **Guest:** UNKNOWN. Membership is required by design: "Il vous suffit de vous inscrire… gratuit et sans engagement". Registration accepts Zalando credentials and Facebook/Gmail/Apple login (help page, `a11y/public_help_membre.yaml`).

## 3. Entry points and navigation
CONFIRMED:
- Header "Compte" opens a dialog with "Votre compte" `/myaccount`, "Aide et FAQ" `/help` and "Déconnexion" `/logout` (not clicked).
- `/myaccount` redirects to `/myaccount/overview`.
- Footer "Rétractation" → `/myaccount/return/selection?withdrawal=true`.

Evidence: `a11y/account_menu.yaml`, `account_overview.yaml`.

- **Outputs:** order and return lists (empty for this account).

## 7. Business rules
- **BR-15-01 CONFIRMED:** membership is free. Zalando group credentials work (shared identity), and social login is offered (help page).
- **BR-15-02 CONFIRMED (CGV §9.4–9.5):** one account per customer, and members must be 18 or older.
- **BR-15-03 CONFIRMED:** newsletter frequency is a choice of daily and/or weekly sales. A separate opt-in allows newsletter tracking and personalisation.
- **BR-15-04 CONFIRMED:** unsubscribing requires a reason (the button is disabled until one is selected) and is scoped to Privé only.
- **BR-15-05 CONFIRMED:** members can save size filters per gender and garment group, in EU/US systems. HYPOTHESIS: they pre-filter listings (M06).
- **BR-15-06 CONFIRMED:** returns are self-service from the account (`/myaccount/return/selection`), with a separate withdrawal entry point (`?withdrawal=true`). CGV §6 says returns use a QR code or label from the account.
- **BR-15-07 CONFIRMED:** save buttons are disabled until a change is made.
