# M14 — Cart (reservation basket)

**Status:** Analysed with user authorization: one item added, inspected and removed. Checkout not entered.
**Labels:** CONFIRMED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN / OUT OF SCOPE.
**Paths:** `a11y/…` = `evidence/a11y/…`. Evidence IDs are in [`evidence/INDEX.md`](../../evidence/INDEX.md). BR-IDs are in [business-rules.md](../requirements/business-rules.md).

## 1. Module purpose
CONFIRMED: A drawer-style cart that holds **reserved** items for a limited time before checkout.

## 2. User roles and actors
- **Member:** CONFIRMED.
- **Other shoppers:** affected indirectly, because reserved units are unavailable to them (BR-13-02).
- **Guest:** UNKNOWN.

## 3. Entry points and navigation
CONFIRMED:
- Header button "Votre panier". There is no separate URL: the page URL stays the same and a `dialog` opens.
- PDP "Ajouter au panier".

Evidence: `a11y/cart_empty.yaml`, `a11y/cart_with_item.yaml`.

## 4. Pages and UI components
CONFIRMED:
- **Dialog title:** "Votre panier - 1 Article - 14 minutes et 7 secondes restantes".
- **Summary:** "Vous économisez 135,00 € Par rapport au prix de vente conseillé"; "Somme totale 115,00 €"; "Taxe sur la valeur ajoutée incluse, hors emballage & manutention".
- **Checkout button:** "Commander (1 Article)". Not clicked: it leads to M21.
- **Group label:** "Réservé".
- **List "Articles dans votre panier":** brand link to the PDP, type, "Taille: 39 1/3", RRP, discount "-54%", price, image, and remove button "Retirez <produit> de votre panier."
- **Close buttons.**
- **Empty state:** "Vous n’avez aucun article réservé dans votre panier."

## 5. User interactions
CONFIRMED:
- Open or close the drawer.
- Remove → **alertdialog "Retirer l’article du panier"** with "Si vous retirez cet article, il sera disponible pour que d’autres acheteurs puissent le réserver." and buttons Supprimer / Annuler / Fermer.
- "Supprimer" → empty cart.
- **Not exercised:** "Commander" (OUT OF SCOPE).

## 6. Inputs and outputs
- **Inputs:** add (SKU + size), remove (with confirmation).
- **Outputs:**
  - item count and remaining reservation time in the header button's accessible name
  - savings vs RRP
  - total incl. VAT, excluding the packaging/handling fee

## 7. Business rules
- **BR-14-01 CONFIRMED:** the cart is a reservation. Items are listed as "Réservé".
- **BR-14-02 CONFIRMED:** the reservation lasts **20 minutes** from adding ("20 minutes et 0 secondes restantes" right after adding, then 16:59 and 14:07 on later reads). The countdown is shown in the header and the cart title.
- **BR-14-03 CONFIRMED:** removing needs explicit confirmation and releases the unit to other shoppers.
- **BR-14-04 CONFIRMED:** the cart shows the total saving vs RRP and the per-line discount %. Here -54% is 115 vs 250 (exactly 54%).
- **BR-14-05 CONFIRMED:** the cart total excludes the packaging/handling fee, which is added at checkout (3,90 € per order per the help page, BR-12-06).
- **BR-14-06 HYPOTHESIS:** when the timer reaches zero the reservation is released and the item leaves the cart. Not observed.

## 8. State transitions
CONFIRMED: `Empty → (add) → Reserved [20:00 countdown] → (remove + confirm) → Empty`. UNKNOWN: `Reserved → (timer 0) → ?` and `Reserved → (Commander) → checkout` (OUT OF SCOPE).

## 9. Dependencies on other modules
M07 (add), M13 (stock), M12 (pricing), M21 (checkout, OUT OF SCOPE), M01 (header badge).

## 10. Observable validation and error states
CONFIRMED: the remove confirmation dialog. UNKNOWN: errors such as the item becoming unavailable while in the cart.

## 11. Responsive behaviour
UNKNOWN. Only a 1280×720 desktop viewport was rendered (see platform-overview §3).

## 12. Unknowns and assumptions
UNKNOWN:
- the multi-item timer rule (does each item have its own timer, or does a new item reset a shared one?)
- a quantity selector (none visible for 1 item)
- vouchers in the cart (CGV §5 says vouchers are entered before the order is sent)
