# M12 — Pricing display & fees

**Status:** Phase 01/02 captures + public CGV/help.
**Labels:** CONFIRMED / HYPOTHESIS / UNKNOWN / OUT OF SCOPE.
**Paths:** `a11y/…` = `evidence/a11y/…`. Evidence IDs are in [`evidence/INDEX.md`](../../evidence/INDEX.md). BR-IDs are in [business-rules.md](../requirements/business-rules.md).

## 1. Module purpose
CONFIRMED: Shows the sale price against the RRP, with legal footnotes and the per-order fee.

## 2. User roles and actors
- **Member (logged-in shopper):** CONFIRMED, as the session used.
- **Guest:** UNKNOWN (not observed).

## 3. Entry points and navigation
All listings, rails, the PDP and the cart.

## 4. Pages and UI components
CONFIRMED:
- **Tiles:** "Prix de vente conseillé <RRP>" + "Prix de vente <price>".
- **Rails:** "prix conseillé <RRP>*" + "<price>¹".
- **PDP:** price with the RRP struck through.
- **Cart:** "Vous économisez …", per-line "-54%", "Somme totale … hors emballage & manutention".
- **Footnotes:** * (vs RRP), ¹ (VAT incl., fees excl., per CGV §2), ³ (required field).

## 5. User interactions
None.

## 6. Inputs and outputs
Prices in EUR with a comma decimal.

## 7. Business rules
- **BR-12-01 CONFIRMED:** discounts are expressed vs the RRP.
- **BR-12-02 CONFIRMED:** prices include VAT (CGV §3.1 "prix définitifs, toutes taxes comprises").
- **BR-12-03 CONFIRMED:** a packaging and handling fee is charged per order (CGV §3.2). It's shown on the order page and may differ for bulky, heavy or valuable items (CGV §3.3).
- **BR-12-04 CONFIRMED (observation):** sale prices end in ,90 / ,00 / ,50. A rounding rule is HYPOTHESIS.
- **BR-12-05 CONFIRMED:** the campaign headline is the maximum discount ("jusqu'à").
- **BR-12-06 CONFIRMED (help page):** the fee is **3,90 € per order, whatever the number of items**.
- **BR-12-07 CONFIRMED (CGV §3.1):** the price charged is the one shown when the order is placed.
- **Note:** the footer footnote cites "§ 2. des CGV" for fees, but the fee clause is §3 in the CGV version of 22.07.2026. That's a reference defect.
