## 7. Business rules
- **BR-17-01 CONFIRMED:** returns are free. The window is stated inconsistently (see BR-17-04).
- **BR-17-02 CONFIRMED:** delivery is shown as a date range. Help: each product has its own delivery time, shown in the product details.
- **BR-17-03 CONFIRMED:** carriers. Help: "Privé by Zalando livre vos commandes en France grâce à Colissimo", Monday to Saturday. The footer also lists Colis Privé.
- **BR-17-04 CONFIRMED (conflict):** three return periods are published:
  - PDP: 31 days from delivery
  - Help: free return of unworn items within **30 days** of receipt
  - CGV §6: **14-day** legal withdrawal; return costs are covered when using the account QR code or label within France
  - PO decision needed (Q-31).
- **BR-17-05 CONFIRMED (CGV §2.7):** delivery only to metropolitan France, Corsica and Monaco. Help: only metropolitan France when logged in on .fr.
- **BR-17-06 CONFIRMED (help):** delivery options are home delivery ("Livraison à domicile") or a relay point ("8 000 nouveaux bureaux"). Express delivery exists only for some articles.
- **BR-17-07 CONFIRMED (help):** items of one order can't ship separately, separate orders can't be merged, and the address can't be changed after the order is validated.
- **BR-17-08 CONFIRMED (CGV §2.5):** the delivery time is counted in working days from receipt of payment.
