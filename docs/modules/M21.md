## 7. Business rules
Public CGV rules (CONFIRMED as **documented**, not observed in a flow):
- **BR-21-01 CONFIRMED (CGV text):** the order becomes final with the "confirmer la commande" button, and an email confirmation follows (§2.3).
- **BR-21-02 CONFIRMED (CGV text):** shipping happens after full payment (advance payment = reservation). Payment is due within 7 days of the order confirmation (§2.4).
- **BR-21-03 CONFIRMED (CGV text):** methods per CGV are advance payment, credit card and PayPal. Some may be withheld per order, and only EU bank accounts are accepted (§4.1). The footer additionally shows Amex, Apple Pay and Klarna.
- **BR-21-04 CONFIRMED (CGV text):** vouchers are free promotional codes with an expiry date. They're single use, one per order, can't be combined, may exclude brands, may have a minimum order, and must be entered before the order is sent. The voucher value isn't refunded on return (§5).
- **BR-21-05 CONFIRMED (CGV text):** invoices are electronic only (§4.4).
- **BR-21-06 CONFIRMED (CGV text):** a claim may be assigned to Zalando Payments GmbH (§2.8).
